What's inside

Flashlight
Torch, strobe, SOS and a screen light in any color, with an auto-off timer.
Concert Mode
Scrolling messages in LED, neon and other styles. Lights can pulse to the music.
Compass
Magnetic heading. Nearby metal and your phone's sensor affect the reading.
Spirit level
Check the angle of a surface. You choose when to set a new zero.
Magnetic field
See changes near magnets and some metals. It cannot find every metal, pipe or wall stud.
Microphone level
Compare the recorded signal and see the pitch. It is not a hearing-safety meter.
Sensors
Live motion, pressure, location, light, proximity and battery readings, when your phone has them.

Questions

Why does the app ask for the camera?

Android and iOS control the flashlight through the camera. The app does not take photos or video.

Can the Magnetic Field tool find metal?

It shows changes in the magnetic field near your phone. Results depend on the sensor and the object. It cannot find every metal, pipe or wall stud.

Does the app collect my data?

Use and crash reports are off until you turn them on in Privacy & data, the shield button on the Flash screen. You can turn them off there at any time. Your messages and favorites stay on your phone. The privacy policy has the details, in English and Bahasa Malaysia.

Why is the compass wrong?

Move your phone in a figure 8 a few times. Keep it away from metal, magnets and phone cases with magnets.

Does it work offline?

Yes, most tools do. A location fix can take longer without a connection. Use and crash reports, if you turn them on, wait until you are online.

How do I report a problem or ask for a feature?

Email aniniwtf@gmail.com. Tell us your phone model and what happened.
